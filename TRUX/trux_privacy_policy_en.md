**Last updated:** 06.06.2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the TRUX mobile application ("the App") collects, uses, stores, and protects user data. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

TRUX is an analysis tool that evaluates news text you provide using an artificial intelligence model and returns a probability-based prediction about the text's credibility, along with an explanation of the words influencing that prediction. TRUX is a decision-support tool; its results are not guaranteed to be accurate.

## 1. Data We Collect

The App only processes the data necessary to perform its function:

**a) Text you submit for analysis**
When you enter news text to analyze, that text is sent to our server (the analysis API) for processing. The text is evaluated by the AI model, and a prediction together with an explanation is returned to you.

**b) Voluntary feedback**
If you choose to send feedback about an analysis result (for example, indicating whether the result was correct or incorrect), this feedback is stored on the server side along with the related text and is used solely to improve the model's accuracy (retraining).

**c) Technical data**
Limited technical information (such as error logs and request timestamps) may be processed to operate and debug the App. This data is not used to identify you.

The App does **not** collect personal identifiers such as your name, phone number, location, contacts, or files on your device.

## 2. How We Use Data

We use the data we collect only for the following purposes:

- To analyze the text you submit and provide you with a prediction and explanation (influential words);
- To use voluntary feedback to improve the model's accuracy over time;
- To detect and fix technical issues in the App.

Your data is **not** used for advertising and is **not** sold to third parties.

## 3. Data Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties, including advertisers or marketers. Data may only be processed in the following limited cases:

- Through the server/hosting infrastructure providers we use to operate the App (solely to provide the service);
