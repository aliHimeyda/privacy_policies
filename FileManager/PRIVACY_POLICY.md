## 1. Overview

File Manager ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is an offline file management
application that helps you browse, organize, copy, move, hide, and clean files
stored on your own device.

We have designed the App to be **privacy-friendly by default**. The App does
**not** collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. All processing
happens **locally on your device**, and your files never leave your device
through the App.

This Privacy Policy explains what the App does and does not do with your
information, which device permissions it uses and why, and the rights you have.

## 4. Permissions and How They Are Used

The App requests only the permissions it needs to function as a file manager.
Each permission is used solely on your device and only to provide the features
described below.

### 4.1 Storage Access (All files access — `MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`)

A file manager's core purpose is to let you view and manage the files on your
device. To do this, the App requests "All files access" so it can:

- Display the folders and files in your device storage
- Open, copy, move, rename, and delete files **that you choose**
- Organize files into "saved", "hidden", and "recent" lists you create
- Calculate folder sizes and item counts to display them in the interface

This access is used **only on your device and only to perform actions you
initiate.** The App does **not** read your files in the background, scan them
for advertising or profiling purposes, or upload them anywhere.

### 4.2 Read / Write External Storage (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`)

On older Android versions, these permissions allow the App to read and write
files in shared storage so the same browsing and file-operation features work
across device versions. They are used exclusively for local file operations you
request.

### 4.3 Biometric Authentication (`USE_BIOMETRIC`)

If you choose to protect the App or specific content (for example, hidden files)
with a biometric lock, the App uses your device's biometric system
(such as fingerprint or face unlock) to verify it is you.

- Biometric verification is handled entirely by your device's operating system.
- The App **never** sees, stores, or transmits your fingerprint, face data, or
  any other biometric information.
- The App only receives a simple "success" or "failure" result from the system.

This feature is optional and only active if you enable it.

## 5. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To work properly and quickly, the App saves some information **on your device
only**. This data is never sent anywhere. It includes:

- App settings and preferences (for example, your selected language)
- Lists you create within the App, such as saved, hidden, and recent items
  (these store file paths so the App can show your lists again)
- Performance caches, such as folder item counts, directory contents, and
  thumbnail caches, used to make browsing faster

This information is stored in the App's private local storage (using on-device
databases and preference storage). It is accessible only to the App on your
device and is removed when you uninstall the App or clear the App's data.

## 9. Data Retention and Deletion

- The App retains locally stored settings, lists, and caches only for as long as
  the App is installed.
- You can clear this data at any time through your device's
  **Settings → Apps → File Manager → Storage → Clear data / Clear cache**.
- **Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all data the App stored on your
  device.**

Please note that deleting a file through the App permanently affects the actual
file on your device, just as it would in any file manager. Files you delete are
removed from your device and may not be recoverable.
